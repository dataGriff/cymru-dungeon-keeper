# itch.io page copy

**Title:** Steward of Annwn
**Tagline:** Arawn rides with the Hunt. Keep his hall until he returns.

## Description

You are the steward of a hall beneath a cairn, keeping it for a lord who is out with the Hunt and has not said when he will be back. The seam is rich, the Coblynau are willing, and the stair is long. Mortals have heard that the lord is away and are coming down it to strike the Heart.

Dig the hall out of living rock. Build a Lair and a Hatchery and the Cŵn Annwn will come; build a vault and a Pwca will come to count it; dig to the water and something will come up out of it. Carry your gold home, pay your creatures on the day, lay a pit where the knight has no choice but to step, and keep the manual open. The hall does not forgive a steward who cannot tell a hound from a hound of men.

Five levels, from the Cairn to the night the Hunt comes home. A sandbox that does not stop. An editor, if you think you can dig better.

## Features

- Nine denizens of Welsh myth, each with a page in the bestiary that fills in as you meet them.
- Mortals who tunnel through rock if you give them no corridor, lose their nerve, and loot the floor.
- Gold that has to be carried, wages that have to be paid, and a Pwca who skims.
- Pits, boulders and cold iron snares, built by Coblynau who complain.
- The Tylwyth Teg, who will sell you a ward on the Gate at a price they consider fair.
- A printable manual, a reference card, and a box you cannot buy.

## Controls

Hand G · Dig D · Lair L · Hatchery H · Treasury T · Pool P · Pit I · Boulder B · Snare S · Space pauses · 1/2/3 speed · R restart · F11 fullscreen. Right-click or long-press unmarks and sells. Click a creature with the Hand or Dig tool to read its page.
